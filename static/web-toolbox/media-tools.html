Frequently Asked Questions

What are online video and audio tools? ▼

Online video and audio tools are browser-based utilities that let you process multimedia files directly in your web browser. They include video downloaders, audio editors, screen recorders, and format converters. Unlike desktop software, these tools require no installation — just open the page and start working. All processing happens locally on your device using modern Web APIs.

How does the M3U8 video downloader work? ▼

The M3U8 downloader parses HLS (HTTP Live Streaming) playlist files that contain references to video segments. It fetches each segment in parallel, combines them, and lets you download the complete video. It supports batch downloading, real-time progress display, and pause/resume — all running in your browser with no server involvement.

Can I record my screen without installing any software? ▼

Yes! Our Screen Recorder uses the browser's built-in Screen Capture API to record your screen, application windows, or browser tabs. It supports optional webcam overlay, microphone audio, and system audio. The recorded video is saved directly to your device as WebM or MP4 — no extensions, no plugins, and no watermarks.

What audio and video formats are supported? ▼

Our tools support a wide range of formats. The Audio Cutter handles MP3, WAV, M4A, FLAC, AAC, and OGG. The File Converter supports Video→GIF, Video→Audio, SVG→PNG, and Image↔Base64 conversions. The Screen Recorder exports WebM and MP4. The M3U8 downloader works with HLS/TS streams.

Do these tools work on mobile devices? ▼

Most of our tools work on mobile browsers. The Audio Cutter, File Converter, and M3U8 Downloader are fully responsive and mobile-friendly. Screen recording may have limited support depending on your mobile browser's capabilities. We recommend using Chrome or Edge on desktop for the best screen recording experience.

Are these tools really free with no ads? ▼

Yes, all 5 video and audio tools are 100% free with no ads, no registration required, no watermarks, and no usage limits. Every tool runs entirely in your browser — your files are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server. There are no hidden fees, no premium tiers, and no data collection.
