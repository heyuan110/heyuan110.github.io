FAQ

How accurate is this?
Prices are the official published API rates as of July 9, 2026. Real bills vary with prompt caching (up to 90% off cached input), batch discounts (50%), and always-on thinking tokens on Fable 5.

Why is Sonnet 5 shown twice?
Anthropic's introductory pricing ($2/$10) runs through August 31, 2026; the standard rate is $3/$15. Both are shown so you can budget past September.

Why is GPT-5.6 marked "restricted"?
As of July 2026 it is limited to ~20 government-approved partners. Prices are vendor-published, included for comparison only.

Tokens vs words?
Budget roughly 750 English words (or ~2,500 characters of code) per 1,000 tokens.
